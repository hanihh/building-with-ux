## Group content before styling and rank the groups

**Impact: HIGH (clear groups reduce scanning and comparison)**

Cluster content by meaning, then choose primary, secondary, and tertiary groups. Express that order with size, weight, color, spacing, placement, and proximity; do not give every metric or block equal emphasis.

**Don't:** Style an unstructured list where every item has the same visual weight.

**Do:** Establish a hero message or metric, keep related support nearby, and place tertiary metadata last.
